Why the font is here instead of on Google Fonts: loading it from
fonts.googleapis.com adds a render-blocking third-party stylesheet plus TLS
handshakes to two extra hosts, which pushed first contentful paint on the
homepage from roughly 120ms to 470ms. Self-hosting removes both.

Bundled: the latin subset of the variable italic, which is all the headline
needs. If the headline ever gains accented or extended characters, also copy
the latin-ext file from Google Fonts.
