What is bundled and why
-----------------------
literata-italic-700-latin.woff2 is the 700 italic, latin subset, with the
variable optical-size axis pinned at 48 (the size the headline renders at).
That takes it from 52 kB to 22 kB. It covers the full latin range, so editing
the headline text is safe; only accented-beyond-latin or non-latin characters
would fall back to Georgia.

Self-hosted rather than loaded from Google Fonts, because the third-party
stylesheet is render-blocking and adds TLS handshakes to two extra hosts.

To regenerate after a headline size change, or to update the font:
  curl "https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Literata:ital,opsz,wght@1,7..72,700"
  # take the latin woff2 from that CSS, then
  python3 -m fontTools.varLib.instancer <file> opsz=48 -o pinned.ttf
  python3 -m fontTools.subset pinned.ttf --unicodes=<latin range> \
      --layout-features='*' --flavor=woff2 --output-file=<out>.woff2
